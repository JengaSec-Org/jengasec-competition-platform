CTFs develop tools.
JengaSec develops judgment.

Most talent pipelines produce professionals assessed individually, within their discipline, under controlled conditions. JengaSec produces something different: technologists who have built an enterprise, defended it under fire, and explained their decisions to a room of executives.

// How It Works

Four phases.
One complete lifecycle.

01
// Phase_01 — Pre-Event
Build
// Construction

Blue teams architect and construct their assigned enterprise before the event: full-stack systems with login screens, APIs, databases and core business logic. Organic vulnerabilities emerge from genuine architectural decisions, not planted traps.

02
// Phase_02 — During Event
Maarifa
// Knowledge

Each day opens with two keynotes: one hard cybersecurity talk, one from the wider industry. Tech does not exist in isolation, and what you hear in the morning directly shapes what gets attacked and defended in the afternoon.

03
// Phase_03 — During Event
Morani Hunting Grounds
// Mastery_Under_Pressure

Red teams execute APT-style attacks on systems they did not build: social engineering, SQL injection, privilege escalation, lateral movement, and attacks on the AI defence agent itself. Blue teams defend in real time. AI assists both sides, but human judgment is what gets scored.

04
// Phase_04 — During Event
Kikao
// Dialogue_&_Reflection

CFOs, CTOs and CISOs forensically review what actually happened. They interrogate blue team architecture and red team attack chains in front of the room. Winners are announced here, followed by open Q&A on entering the industry.
