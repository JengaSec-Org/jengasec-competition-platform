Application Red teams do not submit a proposal, and a place here is not something this platform grants. You earn it by qualifying on Hack The Box, in a team of four, every member aged 18 to 25.

  1. RSVP on Meetup{% if rsvp_closes %}, before {{ rsvp_closes|date:"l j F, H:i" }} EAT{% endif %}. Every member RSVPs individually — it is the record Hack The Box uses to onboard you. Each team gets a dedicated lab prepared in advance, so a late RSVP cannot be accommodated.
  2. Get onboarded. Every member needs an active Hack The Box Enterprise account. Access details are sent at onboarding, along with the qualifying mark.
  3. Play the qualifier. {% if qualifier_opens and qualifier_closes %} One online CTF, {{ qualifier_opens|date:"l j F, H:i" }} to {{ qualifier_closes|date:"l j F, H:i" }} EAT. {% else %} One online CTF on Hack The Box Enterprise, played as a team of four. {% endif %}
  4. Then register. If your team passes we email you to confirm your place and send the registration link. Teams that do not qualify cannot register for the Red side.
