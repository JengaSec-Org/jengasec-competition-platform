{% comment %} The privacy notice at the point of collection. Included wherever a competitor is asked to enter personal data, which is what the right to be informed under the Data Protection Act 2019 means in practice: the notice belongs on the form, not only in a policy document somebody has to go looking for. Section 7 of the code of conduct is the full notice; this is the pointer to it. {% endcomment %}

// DATA_PRIVACY
Your details are held by Strathmore University under its Data Privacy Policy and the Data Protection Act, 2019, and are used only to run this competition: to confirm you are eligible, to form teams, to admit you to the venue, and to judge your team's work. Organisers and judges see them; they are not published and not given to partners. Section 7 of the code of conduct sets out the full notice, including how long we keep your data and how to see, correct or object to it. Requests go to dpo@strathmore.edu.
