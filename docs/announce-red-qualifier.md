Hack The Box has partnered with JengaSec 2026, and the Red side now has one way in: an
online qualifier. This replaces what we previously told you about registration, so please
read it even if you have already registered on the platform.

Your place is not held by your platform registration. You earn it by qualifying.

1. RSVP on the Hack The Box Kenya Meetup, before Saturday 10 October at 12:00 EAT.
   Every member of your team RSVPs individually -- it is the record Hack The Box uses to
   onboard you. Each team is given a dedicated lab prepared in advance, so late RSVPs
   cannot be accommodated.

2. Make sure every member has an active Hack The Box Enterprise account. Access details
   and the qualifying mark are sent at onboarding.

3. Play the qualifier: one online CTF, Sunday 11 October 10:00 EAT to Monday 12 October
   22:00 EAT, as a team of four, every member aged 18 to 25.

4. If your team passes, we email you to confirm your place and send your registration
   link for the in-person sessions at Strathmore University, 14 to 16 October.

Use the same name and email on Meetup, Hack The Box and JengaSec so we can match your
team across the three.

The RSVP link and the full details are on your dashboard. Teams that do not qualify
cannot register for the Red side, so the RSVP deadline on Saturday is the one that
matters right now.
