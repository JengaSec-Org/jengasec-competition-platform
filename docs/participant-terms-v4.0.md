5.1	What happens. The Club and NCBA photograph, film and record the Event on 14, 15 and 16 October 2026, including talks, the Morani floor, Kikao panels, the finals, the prize presentation and the recruiter mixer. Everyone at the Event wears a JengaSec lanyard.
